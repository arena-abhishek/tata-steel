Tata Steel UISL

Message From MD's Desk

It gives me great pride to lead Tata Steel Utilities and Infrastructure Services Limited (UISL), an organisation that has been an integral part of Jamshedpur's journey for over a century. Our purpose is deeply rooted in serving the community, building sustainable infrastructure, and enhancing the quality of life for every citizen we touch.

As the city grows and evolves, our responsibility extends to creating solutions that are smart, sustainable, and future-ready — whether ensuring safe water supply, managing waste responsibly, or introducing renewable energy initiatives. We are committed to shaping Jamshedpur into a model urban centre.

Accomplished Projects

Office — Naval Tata Hockey Academy

A state-of-the-art office complex built for the Naval Tata Hockey Academy, supporting India's premier hockey talent development programme with world-class facilities.

Office Building — Pellet Plant (Tata Steel)

Interior works and construction of office infrastructure within Tata Steel's Pellet Plant facility, delivered on time with high quality standards.

Hostel Flat — Bamnipal

Residential hostel construction providing quality housing for workers and staff in Bamnipal, meeting all safety and habitability standards.

2550 TPD ASU Plant — BOC

Construction of a large-scale Air Separation Unit plant with capacity of 2550 TPD for BOC, a landmark industrial EPC project.

Duplex — Park Enclave

Luxury duplex residential construction at Park Enclave, combining modern aesthetics with structural excellence for premium living.

252 Residential Flats — Kadma (Tata Steel)

Mass-scale residential construction of 252 flats at Kadma for Tata Steel employees, delivered with quality finishes and modern amenities.

Hall of Fame

Excellence in Consistent TPM Commitment Award

Tata Steel UISL was conferred with the "Excellence in Consistent TPM Commitment" Award from Japan Institute of Plant Maintenance (JIPM) in a prestigious ceremony held on 21st March 2023 at Kyoto, Japan — recognising outstanding manufacturing and maintenance practices.

CII Water Management Award

Recognised by the Confederation of Indian Industry for exemplary water management practices, conservation initiatives, and sustainable water recycling programmes across Jamshedpur operations.

Significant Achievement Award 2018

Awarded for significant contributions to infrastructure development, operational excellence, and community service in the Jamshedpur region, setting new benchmarks in industrial city management.

ICQCC Excellence Award 2024

Recognised at the International Convention on Quality Control Circles for outstanding quality improvement initiatives and team-based problem solving across operational departments.

TPM Commitment Award 2024

Continued recognition for Total Productive Maintenance excellence, affirming UISL's sustained commitment to operational efficiency, equipment reliability, and workforce capability building.

Best Customer Award 2018

Honoured for exemplary customer relations, stakeholder engagement, and service delivery excellence, reflecting the organisation's commitment to citizen-first utility management.
